# 🇮🇳 India Sales Performance Analysis | Tableau Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **10,000 sales transactions across India** to understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, product performance, and business growth opportunities.

The project was developed as a **Business Intelligence and Business Analyst portfolio project**, using **Excel for data preparation and Tableau for interactive visualization and analysis**.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that can support better decisions around regional performance, product strategy, sales growth, and profitability.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 🥇 Regional Performance

The **North region** is the strongest sales contributor.

* North Sales: **₹37.94M**
* West Sales: **₹25.89M**
* South Sales: **₹25.61M**
* East Sales: **₹13.54M**

The North region contributes approximately **36.8% of total sales**, making it an important market for continued investment.

### 🗺️ State Performance

The leading states by sales include:

1. West Bengal — ₹13.54M
2. Maharashtra — ₹13.46M
3. Delhi — ₹12.95M
4. Tamil Nadu — ₹12.90M
5. Karnataka — ₹12.70M

This provides management with a clear view of the strongest geographic markets.

### 🛍️ Category Performance

The three major categories have relatively balanced sales performance:

* Technology — ₹34.49M
* Furniture — ₹34.33M
* Office Supplies — ₹34.15M

Technology is the leading category by sales, although the difference between categories is relatively small.

### 💰 Profitability

The business generated:

**₹17.89M total profit**

with an overall profit margin of:

**17.38%**

This indicates that the business is generating a healthy positive return from its sales activity.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen the North Region

Since North is the highest-performing region, management should consider expanding successful products, campaigns and customer strategies in this market.

### 2. Investigate East Region Performance

The East region has substantially lower sales than the other regions. Further analysis should investigate customer demand, distribution, pricing and product availability.

### 3. Maintain Balanced Category Investment

Since the three categories contribute similar sales, management should avoid over-dependence on one category while identifying high-margin sub-categories for additional investment.

### 4. Focus on High-Performing States

Top-performing states should receive targeted campaigns, inventory planning and customer retention initiatives.

### 5. Monitor Profit Alongside Sales

High sales do not automatically mean high profitability. Management should track **Sales + Profit + Profit Margin** together when evaluating product and regional performance.
